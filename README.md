  # Retail Sales Analysis

## Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing retail sales data to understand sales performance, profit, customers, products, and business trends.

## Objectives

• Analyze total sales and profit  
• Understand customer and order performance  
• Identify top-performing products  
• Compare sales across different cities and categories  
• Analyze monthly sales trends  
• Calculate important business KPIs  

## Tools Used

• Power BI  
• DAX  
• Data Cleaning & Transformation  
• Data Modeling  

## Key KPIs

• Total Sales  
• Total Profit  
• Total Orders  
• Profit Margin  
• Average Order Value  
• Sales per Customer  

## Dashboard Analysis

• Sales & Profit by Category  
• Sales by Payment Method  
• Monthly Sales & Profit Trend  
• Sales by City  
• Top Products by Sales  
• Top Customers by Sales  
• Sales by Sub-Category  
• Profit by Sub-Category  

## Key Insights

• Identified high-performing categories and products.  
• Compared sales and profit across different locations.  
• Analyzed monthly sales and order trends.  
• Identified important customer and product performance patterns.
